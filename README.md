# HR-Attrition-Analytics-Dashboard
 An end-to-end HR Analytics dashboard built in Power BI, using PostgreSQL as the data source, to analyze employee attrition patterns across 10,000+ employee records and provide actionable retention insights for HR decision-making.

 ##  Business Problem

The company lacked visibility into how many employees were leaving in a given period, which departments/roles were most affected, and what factors were driving attrition. This made it difficult to plan retention strategies, budgets, and workforce planning effectively.

##  Tools & Technologies

- *Power BI* — Dashboard design & visualization
- *PostgreSQL* — Data source / database
- *DAX* — KPI measures and calculations
- *Power Query* — Data cleaning, transformation (ETL)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|

##  Business Questions Answered

| # | Business Problem | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Company had no visibility into overall attrition | Enables workforce planning, budgeting, and retention KPIs |
| 2 | Which departments face the highest attrition? | Enables targeted, department-specific retention strategies |
| 3 | How does job role influence attrition? | Redesign job responsibilities/training for high-attrition roles |
| 4 | Does distance from home correlate with attrition? | Flexible/remote work policies; informs site selection & transport support |
| 5 | Does overtime contribute to attrition? | Promotes balanced workloads and compensation for extra hours |
| 6 | How do job & environment satisfaction affect retention? | Prioritizes HR initiatives — culture, facilities, flexibility, role change |
| 7 | Are low work-life balance employees more likely to quit? | Wellness programs, work-from-home, flexible timing |
| 8 | Are frequent travelers more likely to leave? | Policy restructuring — virtual meetings, travel rotations |
| 9 | Does tenure in the same role affect attrition? | Career pathing, mentorships, training, role changes |
| 10 | Do salary hikes influence retention? | Aligns compensation strategy with retention outcomes |

##  Key Insights

- Overall attrition rate stands at *22.47%, with **Data Science* and *Cyber Security* departments showing the highest attrition (~19–22%).
- *64%* of attrition came from employees who did *not* work overtime, indicating attrition is influenced by more than just workload.
- Attrition varies notably by *job role, **salary hike percentage, and **distance from home*.

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive *KPI cards*: Total Employees, Total Attrition, Active Attrition, Attrition Rate, Average Rate
- *Dynamic slicers*: Job Role, Job Level, Gender
- Visual breakdowns: Department, Job Role, Education, Salary Hike %, Work-Life Balance, Job Satisfaction, Job Environment, Business Travel, Years in Current Role, Distance from Home
